# DSO Redesign

**Author: Trevor Hardy**
**Last Updated: Jan 30, 2025**

This folder contains design ideas for DSO operation based on the experience gained during the DSO+T study and in preparation for a larger re-work of the DSO. None of this should be considered definitive but rather a reflection of the thinking at the time it was created. Later design work may or may not reference this or build upon on depending on how thinking at that time is consistent with the ideas expressed here.

Given those caveats, the thinking about how a DSO should be structured and operate is a reflection of work done over a number of years by multiple people. It is well-reasoned and is should be part of any future thinking, architecting and designing.
